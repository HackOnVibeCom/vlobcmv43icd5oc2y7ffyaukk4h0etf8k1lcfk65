## 1. What does your application/service do?

**PinchForge** is an autonomous Mobile App Growth & Distribution Operating System that turns any mobile store listing (Google Play or Apple App Store URL), PDF brief, or description into an end-to-end multi-channel acquisition and retention campaign in seconds.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Instant Store Ingestion & Multi-Channel Copy:** Extracts real app metadata, screenshots, and formatted store ratings (`4.8 ★`) to generate 6 native pitches for **App Store, Google Play, Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Product Hunt** with strict character limit enforcement.
- **Interactive Feed Mockup Studio (Modal):** Live fullscreen simulation of Twitter, Instagram, and LinkedIn feed cards with 1-click manual upload links (`twitter.com/intent/tweet`, etc.) that copy post text directly to the clipboard.
- **Standalone HTML Microsite Compiler & Fullscreen Sandbox:** Compiles a production-ready, responsive landing page embedded with verified player reviews and device mockups. Features an isolated fullscreen interactive sandbox (Desktop vs Mobile toggle) and 1-click downloadable `.html` export.
- **Paid Acquisition & Apple Search Ads (ASA) Bulk CSV Exporter:** Models category-benchmarked CPI economics ($0.38 blended) and exports an authentic CSV formatted for direct upload to Apple Search Ads Campaign Manager.
- **Pure 3D Studio Visual Assets & QR Standee Studio:** Generates 8k resolution 3D isometric product renders with zero AI text hallucination, plus printable table standees with embedded app emblems.
- **Phase 2: In-App Virality & Retention SDK:** Production-ready code snippets in **Swift (iOS/SwiftUI), Kotlin (Android), Flutter (Dart), and React Native** for viral referral loops, in-app banners, changelogs, and review responder drafts.

## 2. Who is the target audience?

1. **Indie Mobile Developers & Solo Founders:** Shipping apps without a dedicated marketing, ASO, or design team. PinchForge automates store optimization, launch copy, ads setup, and landing page creation in one pass.
2. **Small App Studios & Growth Teams:** Needing repeatable, automated launch pipelines and ongoing release changelogs for bi-weekly app updates.
3. **Freelance App Marketers & Agencies:** Managing multi-app campaign history, category benchmarks, and cross-channel distribution.

- **Primary:** United States, Canada, United Kingdom — the largest mobile app economies with high concentrations of indie developers and venture-backed mobile startups.

## 4. Who are your competitors?

| Competitor Category | Limitations Compared to PinchForge |
|---|---|
| **Generic AI Wrappers** (*ChatGPT, Jasper, Copy.ai*) | Prompt-only wrappers with no store scraping, no character limits, no ASO scoring, and no downloadable ASA CSV or HTML landing pages. |
| **ASO Platforms** (*AppTweak, Sensor Tower*) | High cost ($100–$500/mo), complex enterprise analytics that don't generate creative copy, mockups, or landing pages. |
| **Social Schedulers** (*Buffer, Hootsuite*) | Distribution-only tools that generate zero creative content or app store collateral. |

**PinchForge Advantage:** The only unified platform bridging the gap between app store scraping, creative copy generation, standalone microsite compilation, Apple Search Ads bulk exports, and native in-app virality SDKs.
