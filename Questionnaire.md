## 1. What does your application/service do?

PITCHFORGE takes any app — identified by a store URL, an uploaded brief, or a manual description — and generates launch-ready marketing copy for six platforms simultaneously: App Store, Google Play, Twitter/X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and Product Hunt. Each output is character-limit-aware and platform-native in tone.

Beyond generation, PITCHFORGE runs a deterministic ASO scoring engine (zero AI calls, rules-based, auditable), a 10-check launch readiness checklist, a greedy-algorithm iOS keyword field packer, a category benchmark score, and a "why this copy" reasoning panel that shows exactly which extracted signals shaped each output. It generates matching social preview images per platform, auto-publishes to Discord via user-owned webhooks, and provides one-click share paths to WhatsApp, Telegram, Twitter, Email, Facebook, and Reddit.

Post-launch, it generates changelog / "What's New" copy per platform from a version number and change summary, and drafts tone-matched developer responses to store reviews. All of this works without an account.

---

## 2. Who is the target audience?

Indie developers, small app studios, and solo growth marketers who are launching or actively managing a mobile or web app without a dedicated ASO specialist or social copywriter. They ship fast, they wear many hats, and they currently either skip most of their store and social marketing or do it manually one platform at a time. PITCHFORGE gives them the full launch surface in one pass.

Secondary audience: freelance app marketers and growth agencies managing multiple apps — the multi-campaign history and cross-app pattern insights are built specifically for this use case.

## 3. Which countries are the expected buyers of this service?

Primary: United States, Canada, United Kingdom — the three largest English-speaking app economies with the highest concentration of indie developers and small studios.

Secondary: English-speaking developer communities in Australia, EU (particularly Germany, France, Netherlands), and India's tech-export segment. The 7-language generation feature (English, Spanish, French, Hindi, German, Portuguese, Japanese) directly supports localised launches in these markets without a separate translation step.

## 4. Who are your competitors?

Three categories, none of which do what PITCHFORGE does end-to-end:

**General AI copy tools** (Jasper, Copy.ai, ChatGPT) — generate text but have no app-store-specific rules, no ASO scoring, no character-limit awareness per platform, and no publishing capability. They're prompt wrappers. PITCHFORGE is not.

**ASO platforms** (AppTweak, Sensor Tower, MobileAction) — strong on keyword research and market data, but they don't generate copy, don't touch social platforms, and cost $100–$500/month. Built for large teams, not indie developers.

**Social scheduling tools** (Buffer, Hootsuite) — handle publishing but generate nothing. They assume you already have the copy.

No existing tool combines store-specific copy generation, deterministic quality scoring, social publishing, and ongoing post-launch tools (changelog, review responses) in a single flow.

---

## 5. What is your advantage?

Three things that a plain AI prompt cannot replicate:

**Deterministic quality engines.** The ASO scoring engine, launch checklist, and iOS keyword packer are rules engines — zero AI calls, same input always produces the same output, every result is auditable with a visible reason. A judge can see exactly why a score is an A or a C, and exactly what to fix. No other team in the field had this.

**Real closed-loop publishing.** PITCHFORGE doesn't just generate copy — it publishes it. Discord auto-publish via user-owned webhooks requires no OAuth setup on our side and no API keys from the user beyond a paste from their channel settings. Engagement data fed back into the pattern insights engine creates a genuine learning loop. This is the "watch it promote itself" feature Illia described as the ideal product.

**Post-launch recurring value.** The changelog generator and review response drafter extend the product beyond a one-time launch tool into something a developer uses every release cycle. The changelog tool alone answers the "would people use it again?" question directly — it's needed every single time an app ships an update, which for active apps is every 2–4 weeks. No copy tool addresses this. It's the strongest retention hook in the product.
